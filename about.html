Our Story

Built at the intersection of creativity and operations.

Great ideas deserve the structure, care, and collaboration required to bring them fully to life.

PMA was created to close the gap between vision and execution. We work alongside clients, creatives, organizations, and community partners to shape ideas, guide the process, and make every moving piece feel intentional.

Our role is both creative and practical: listening closely, building the right team, establishing a clear path forward, and staying present from the first conversation through the final experience.

Based in Washington, DC, we believe the best work is rooted in trust, strengthened by community, and measured by how people feel when the work is done.

Meet the Founder

Leadership grounded in connection.

Paul is a creative operations leader who knows how to turn a strong idea into an experience people remember.

His approach combines strategic thinking, relationship building, and hands-on execution. Whether he is guiding a project, producing an event, or bringing partners together, Paul leads with curiosity, transparency, and a deep respect for every person contributing to the work.

Through PMA, he creates the conditions for creative teams to do their best work—supported by a process that feels organized, collaborative, and human.
