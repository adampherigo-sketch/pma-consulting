A Network Built on Trust

The right people for the right project.

Exceptional events and projects rarely happen because of one person or one organization. They happen when the right people bring their strengths together.

PMA Consulting maintains relationships with trusted professionals across event production, creative services, hospitality, entertainment, community organizations, professional services, and specialized support.

Rather than trying to be everything ourselves, we build the right team around each project's needs, goals, audience, and vision.

How We Work Together

Building the right team around the work.

Every project is different. PMA approaches collaboration intentionally, bringing people together based on the project's actual needs rather than forcing every client into the same formula.

01

Understand

We begin with the client's goals, audience, budget, scope, priorities, and vision for the experience.

02

Connect

We identify trusted professionals, businesses, and organizations whose expertise fits the project's needs.

03

Coordinate

We help bring those moving pieces together around one vision and one cohesive client experience.
